GroupUp

Sixteen words. Four hidden groups. Can you find them?

How to play

  • Pick four words that have something in common, then press Submit.
  • A group can be anything: places, people, phrases, wordplay. Look out for words that seem to fit more than one group.
  • You start with {{ scoring.start }} points. Each wrong guess costs {{ scoring.mistake }}, but you can keep guessing as long as you like.
  • The clock starts when you press Start. Finish in under {{ scoring.bonus_seconds }} seconds for a time bonus of up to {{ scoring.bonus_max }} points. It stays full for the first {{ scoring.grace_seconds }} seconds, then drops by {{ scoring.bonus_per_second }} points every second.
